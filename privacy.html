Daymark Social Studio

Privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026. Sumanth Karasala operates Daymark Social Studio. For privacy questions, email info@aximary.com.

How the app works

The iOS app connects to the Daymark workspace service address that you configure. This release does not provide an Aximary-hosted Daymark service. The app does not include advertising, tracking, or a developer-operated analytics service. Planning a post in this release does not publish it to a social network.

Workspace information

Your configured workspace service can store post drafts, captions, schedules, media you import, sample channel information, and workspace settings. These records support the planning functions you request. The service operator controls their storage and access. Connected social accounts and live publishing are unavailable in this iOS release.

Choices and deletion

You can edit or delete drafts and media in the workspace. For deletion of a workspace or questions about a service you operate, contact its operator. For questions about this app or a support message you sent us, email info@aximary.com. Please do not send passwords or private workspace content in support email.

We will update this page before offering a developer-hosted workspace or changing the app's data practices.
